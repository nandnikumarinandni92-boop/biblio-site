The Psychology of Money

by Morgan Housel

About the book

The psychology of money explains that managing money is not only about mathematics or earning a high income . it also depends on our behavior , patience , decision and attitude toward wealth . The book presents financial lessons through stories and examples that help readers understand the relationship between money, happiness, risk, and personal freedom.

Main points

1.Money is about behavior

financial success depends not only on knowledge but also on our habits,emotions,and decisions ">

2. Luck and risk play a role

financial outcomes are influenced by factors beyond our control, and we should be aware

3. Save for freedom

saving can give you more choices and greater control over your future

4. Avoid comparing yourself with others

everyone has different circumstances and goals. Focus on your own journey

5. Be patient and think long-term

building wealth takes time, and compounding works best when you give it time to grow

6. Avoid debt and live below your means

living within your means and avoiding debt can help you build financial security and reduce stress
