ikigai

A different approach to living a meaningful life.

About the Book

This book explores how choosing what truly matters can help us focus our energy on the things that are important in life.

Main points

1. Find your purpose

discover activities and values that give your life meaning and direction

2. Discover your passion

identify what you love doing and how it aligns with your values

3. Serve others

find ways to contribute to your community and make a positive impact

4. Live in the present moment

focus on the here and now, rather than dwelling on the past or worrying about the future

Key Idea

We cannot care deeply about everything. Learning to choose our priorities can help us deal with problems more calmly and live with greater purpose.
